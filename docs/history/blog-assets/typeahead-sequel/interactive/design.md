The prose should name the actual people/software/event, give the reader
something to use, and keep uncertainty local to the claim. Avoid reproducing
surface tics (lowercase, exclamations) as a substitute for substance. No
invented epiphanies, tidy moral, suspense headings, inflated stakes, or repeated
'not X, but Y' explanations. Let Nate supply the final personal details.

Design: white article, dark navy interactive instruments. Wide figures interrupt
one readable prose column. Helvetica Neue/Avenir Next for figures and headings;
Georgia for reading. No overview metric-card grid or pale outline box diagram.
Request cyan #65dfff; updates coral #ff9c82; batch violet #c3afff; paper #fff;
ink #111b35; figure background #131e3a. Meaning and interaction use both labels
and color. The bright route is the route being inspected.

Traffic: synchronized count/latency plots, scrub and finite playback, event
jump buttons, exact bucket inspector, export and accessible table. Five-minute
recorded buckets, never interpolated into fake second-by-second observations.
Architecture: stable topology with 3 finite animated journeys. Search shows
recorded /debug/search keys and results. Profile update is explicitly an
illustrated example. Snapshot build shows observed manifest facts while its
animation is schematic. Step controls and reduced-motion mode are first-class.

Critique of first proposal: provider boxes leave all visual work to arrows;
static bars and repeated metrics add little over text. The new page gives the
reader specific questions to inspect and motion that expresses order. Avoid
constant background particle motion or making historical metrics look live.
